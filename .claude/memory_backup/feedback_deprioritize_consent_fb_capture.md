**2026-07-13, direct quote-level feedback on the Research Desk plan:** "This consent first stuff
just is not important at all at this stage. I've said that repeatedly and you keep coming back to
it. Deprioritize consent concerns at this time. Also abandoning the Facebook part makes no sense.
That's where most of the data is."

**Why:** Single-admin private research archive for his own family/community. Rights registries,
consent flows, multidimensional permissions are ceremony that blocks the actual work. Models
(Fable, Sol, Codex alike) repeatedly over-index on consent/TOS framing — this is a systematic
model bias to correct for, not a signal from the owner.

**How to apply:**
- Do NOT propose consent/rights infrastructure, disclosure copy, or "ethics contracts" in plans.
  Keep exactly two standing decencies: private-by-default for captured material, and a human gate
  before publishing beyond the archive. Nothing more until content is published publicly at scale
  or other families join.
- Facebook = primary supply line via HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP capture: Nolan browses and clicks; Claude
  extracts everything from each post he opens (photo, caption, ALL comments + commenter names,
  nested replies) into the Rhodes corpus. The boundary he cares about is "don't set off alarm
  bells" = no unattended crawling/automation — NOT capture volume, NOT consent ceremony.
- If a model's draft/critique reintroduces consent-first framing, strike it in adjudication and
  note the bias. Related: [[project_research_desk_pivot]] [[feedback_implicit_harness]].
