## The mystery
A young man appears in **two** photographs from the Charles Fox (Dayton, Ohio) collection — an
outdoor group outing, WWI-era. In both frames he stands with **Albert Fox** and **Irving Fox**. He
has never been identified. He was once mislabeled "Harry Fox" and detached. **Who is he?**

- Photo `01659` — the young man, **Albert Fox** (confirmed), **Irving Fox** (confirmed).
- Photo `02068` — the same young man, Albert, Irving, and three still-unidentified people.

## The evidence (each point is a numbered card the models were given)

**It's the same man in both photos.** The two faces match at L2 = 0.629 (calibrated 0.914) — same
person, high confidence (E1).

**The face matcher can't name him.** Searched against all ~3,285 faces in the archive, his nearest
non-self match is L2 = 1.208 — different-person territory. Every top-20 candidate is ≥ 1.20. The
closest *confirmed* people are Fox/Burd relatives, but all at weak, non-committal distances. The
matcher's honest answer is "family-adjacent, nobody specific" (E2).

**He runs with the Fox brothers.** In both photos he's beside Albert Fox (b. ~1896) and Irving Fox
(b. 1898) — who share parents, so they're brothers (E3-E6). In 1917-18 they're ~21 and ~19. He reads
as a peer of that generation.

**When & where.** Clothing and setting date both photos to ~1915-1922, an outdoor park; one photo
shows a conservatory in the background (E8). The conservatory is identified as **Belle Isle, Detroit**
— it matches Library of Congress LC-DIG-det-4a17798 plus six corroborating sources, so the *location*
is strong (E10). The tighter **1917-18** dating rests on Albert Fox's Detroit residence and 1918 draft
induction and is a **lead still to re-verify**, not settled fact (E11).

**A contradiction worth seeing.** The database's *current* location estimates still say "United
States" (01659) and "New York City" (02068) — stale, and at odds with the Detroit research above
(E9 vs E10-E11).

**The GEDCOM candidate on file looks wrong.** The identity currently carries a weak (0.30) candidate
link to **Harry Isaackovitz** (b. 1881). He'd be ~36-37 here — not a peer of two 20-year-olds — and
no reference photo of him exists to compare against (E7, E12).

## Your three calls (make them before you reveal)
1. **Who is he** — a Fox sibling/relative of Albert & Irving, someone outside the family, or *abstain*?
2. **Keep or drop** the Harry Isaackovitz (b.1881) candidate link?
3. **What one piece of evidence** would most cheaply settle it?
