# Session 100 Antigravity Plan Review Prompt

Use `docs/prds/040_multi_community_bootstrap_and_face_cards.md` as the primary
plan and `docs/session_context/session-100-context.md` as the required context.
Read `docs/assessments/session-100-codex-research.md` before reviewing.

This is a docs-only critical review pass. Do not implement app code.

## Your Job

Critically evaluate the Session 100 plan for:
1. repo accuracy
2. scope discipline
3. visual/interaction ambition
4. multi-face face-card quality
5. risk of regressions
6. whether the Codex ownership split vs. Antigravity ownership split is correct
7. adoption, discoverability, and first-run contributor/admin clarity

## What To Produce

1. Create `docs/assessments/session-100-antigravity-plan-review.md`
2. Update `docs/session_logs/session-100-planning-log.md`

## Review Standards

Be specific and repo-backed.

I want:
1. Findings first, ordered by severity.
2. Exact places where the plan is too vague, too risky, or not ambitious enough.
3. Explicit comment on whether the face-card strategy is good enough for dense
   multi-face identities.
4. Explicit comment on whether the community bootstrap plan is sufficient to let
   Nolan launch another archive soon.
5. Explicit comment on whether a first-time visitor can understand where they
   are, what archive they are in, and how to contribute.
6. Explicit comment on whether the proposed public flows help or hurt adoption.
5. Clear separation between:
   - Antigravity critique
   - Codex-authored planning
   - collaborative/handoff boundary

## Constraints

1. No implementation code changes.
2. No data changes.
3. No branch-spread work.
4. Do not rewrite the Codex plan in place.
5. If you disagree with a major plan decision, say so plainly and propose the
   alternative in the review artifact.
6. Do not default to generic component-library answers; stay Rhodesli-specific.

## Required Sections In Your Review

1. Verdict
2. Findings
3. What I agree with
4. What I would change before implementation
5. Suggested Antigravity-owned work if Session 100 proceeds
6. Attribution
7. Adoption / Discoverability Risks
8. What should be more obvious to a first-time user
9. What should be removed because it adds clutter

## Adoption Questions You Must Answer

1. Does the neutral root create a better top-of-funnel than the current Rhodes
   default?
2. Will a non-expert family member know how to get from landing page to useful
   contribution?
3. Does the face-card plan improve picture legibility, or just add more UI?
4. Where are the likely dead ends or moments of confusion?
5. What is the smallest change that would most improve adoption?

## Logging

Update `docs/session_logs/session-100-planning-log.md` with:

Do not say the plan is ready unless you can defend that claim against:
- zero-regression expectations
